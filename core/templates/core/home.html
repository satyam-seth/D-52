How It Works

Step 1
Create a Room

Room admins can easily create a new room and invite roommates to join. Set up your shared space in just a few clicks.

Step 2
Invite Roommates

Send invitations to your roommates via email. Once they join, you can all start managing your expenses together.

Step 3
Add Expenses

Room members can add daily expenses and specify who paid. It will automatically split the costs and keep track of who owes what.

Step 4
Settle Balances

View outstanding balances and settle up with ease. Keep everyone in the loop and ensure a fair share of expenses.

Step 5
Download Excel Report

Generate detailed expense reports in Excel format. Download them with a single click for easy sharing and analysis.

Step 6
Get Insights

Gain insights into your spending habits. Use our analytics tools to see where your money goes and make informed decisions.

Step 7
Notification Updates

Stay informed with real-time notifications. Get alerts for new expenses, balance updates, and reminders for pending payments.
